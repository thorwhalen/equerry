# Cross-domain events

Some asks are events that touch several teams at once. Small companies handle them with one named owner per item (the "directly responsible individual") and a checklist per recurring event. Here the **secretary is the DRI** (in a host without one, the calling session is): it opens one item per team touched, each with its team's lines, and closes the event when every item is closed. Teams do not message each other; the DRI carries what one team needs from another.

How to run one: name the event and its date; copy the event's checklist below; give each line to the team in brackets as an ask through `equerry-team` (one coverage line per team); collect the results under one heading in the brief; close the event when the last item closes.

This file is the only copy: each domain skill points here for its items. Jurisdiction labels: `[US]`, `[FR]`, `[EU]`. A number `[n]` is source n of the research report's REFERENCES list, the same number in every team's `equerry-<team>/references/sources.md`; re-verify any date-bound line there before relying on it. Last checked 2026-11-09.

## Equity grant

Teams: people, legal, finance, ventures.

- [ ] [legal] board or shareholder approval on file; grant documents drafted by counsel from the plan's form
- [ ] [people] grant agreement and vesting terms written; `[US]` 83(b) filed within 30 days if shares vest [11]; `[FR]` BSPCE conditions checked [22]; the recipient's record updated by proposal
- [ ] [finance] `[US]` a current 409A for the grant date, checked with ventures [13]; tax consequences noted for the professional
- [ ] [ventures] cap table updated on the venture page; `[US]` QSBS holding-period and `[FR]` BSPCE dates noted [14]

## Fundraise (SAFE or priced round)

Teams: ventures, legal, finance, people.

- [ ] [ventures] instrument and template chosen (post-money SAFE or priced round), with the reasons, noted on the page [38][39]; data room assembled and current; investor-update list updated
- [ ] [legal] the brief to counsel; `[US]` Form D and state notices calendared after the first sale [12]
- [ ] [finance] the cash and runway effect; the pack and data-room numbers; `[US]` 409A refreshed after the round [13]
- [ ] [people] founder and key-person agreements and IP assignments in order for diligence [28][29]; any option pool change reflected in offers in flight

## New consulting or fractional client

Teams: bizdev, legal, finance, people, ventures.

- [ ] [bizdev] proposal and SOW per `equerry-bizdev` checklist 2, rate from the card; pipeline entry closed as won, with the scope and the rate
- [ ] [legal] conflict check against the venture map: non-competes, IP assignments and confidentiality the principal already owes [30][31]
- [ ] [legal] master agreement and SOW: the principal's background IP listed in a schedule and retained, deliverables' IP stated, out-of-scope written
- [ ] [finance] tax forms exchanged (`[US]` W-9 or W-8); invoicing entity; `[FR]` `[EU]` VAT treatment; payment terms from the contract register
- [ ] [people] subcontractor papers if anyone else works on it: classification, IP assignment flowing down to the client's terms, confidentiality
- [ ] [ventures] a page for the engagement (role `client`) with its time budget; conflict map updated with legal [30]

## New consulting or training offer

Teams: bizdev, marketing, finance.

- [ ] [bizdev] the offer: its buyer, its scope and its price from the rate card
- [ ] [finance] the offer's effective day rate, counting unbilled preparation and sales time, against the rate card
- [ ] [marketing] a positioning page for the offer; where it will be said

## Training engagement in France

Teams: bizdev, legal, finance.

- [ ] [bizdev] `[FR]` funding route known (Qualiopi certification is required for OPCO or CPF funding), per `equerry-bizdev` checklist 4
- [ ] [legal] `[FR]` the training contract; the training-provider activity declaration on file
- [ ] [finance] `[FR]` data for the annual pedagogical and financial report (BPF) captured as each session is delivered [23]; the funding route's invoicing rules, with bizdev

## A new product idea worth a bet

Teams: product, engineering, bizdev, legal.

- [ ] [product] a pitch per `equerry-product` checklist 1, with the kill line
- [ ] [engineering] feasibility, and the dispatch to a working session if the bet is taken
- [ ] [bizdev] who would pay, and through which offer
- [ ] [legal] whenever users' personal data, children or a licence are involved

## Product launch

Teams: product, marketing, legal, engineering, finance, people, ventures, bizdev.

- [ ] [product] problem statement, bet and kill-or-continue date current and written before launch; what success looks like at that date
- [ ] [marketing] positioning (the five components) written before any launch copy [45]; `equerry-marketing` checklist 1; announcement drafted
- [ ] [legal] `[EU]` privacy notice and record of processing current [32]; `[US]` COPPA if any user may be under 13 [33]; `[US]` FERPA if sold to schools [57]; `[EU]` AI Act transparency duties if it has AI features [37]
- [ ] [engineering] security baseline (`equerry-engineering` checklist 2) on what ships; the release passed the host's code and security reviewers; store compliance (checklist 5) for an app
- [ ] [finance] app-store or marketplace tax set-up, and what the store remits [54]; new recurring costs added to the admin estate
- [ ] [people] contributors' IP assignments complete for what ships
- [ ] [ventures] the product's page: stage, kill signals, teams engaged
- [ ] [bizdev] who sells it, at what price, through which offer

## Open-source release or licence change

Teams: engineering, legal, marketing, ventures.

- [ ] [engineering] licence from the OSI list and REUSE-compliant headers (`equerry-engineering` checklist 1); contributor terms chosen (DCO or CLA)
- [ ] [engineering] [legal] `[EU]` Cyber Resilience Act scope memo (engineering checklist 4) if a venture monetises the project [35][36]; licence and contributor terms reviewed by legal; a licence change on a released project goes to counsel (escalation line)
- [ ] [marketing] release notes and announcement written and scheduled; the README as the library's landing page
- [ ] [ventures] the open-source page's sources and teams engaged updated

## New contractor

Teams: people, legal, finance.

- [ ] [people] classification tested: `[US]` common-law control test, `[FR]` subordination test (requalification is retroactive) [26][27]
- [ ] [legal] [people] IP assignment and confidentiality signed before work starts [28][29]
- [ ] [finance] `[US]` W-9 or W-8 collected at onboarding and the 1099-NEC obligation noted [25]; payment set up
- [ ] [people] access granted least-privilege, listed, with an offboarding or review date

## Taking a new role in a venture

Teams: ventures, legal, finance, people.

- [ ] [ventures] role documents (offer, board appointment, advisory agreement) collected; the page created or updated: role, rights and duties, time budget
- [ ] [legal] corporate-opportunity and conflict review against the other ventures; conflict map updated [30][31]
- [ ] [finance] tax consequences of any equity noted for the accountant; expenses and invoicing arrangements
- [ ] [people] the dossier's roles field proposed for update

## Year end

Teams: all.

- [ ] [legal] [finance] next year's statutory and tax calendar generated from the two teams' `references/calendar.md` and the venture pages; the admin estate's yearly renewals listed
- [ ] [ventures] every venture page reviewed; kill signals and review dates renewed; time budgets reset with the principal
- [ ] [people] people register reviewed; agreements up for yearly review listed; the principal's facts past review listed
- [ ] every team: engagement letter re-read where it no longer fits (its optional `review_suggested` date is a hint); edit it or pause it
- [ ] every team: `equerry-team-review` run, with the year's false-coverage rate
