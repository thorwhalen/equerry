# Marketing: starter sources

Numbers are those of the REFERENCES list of the framework's research report `docs/research/domain-experts-for-a-solo-technical-founder.md`, so they match across every team's sources. Kind: primary (the authority itself), secondary (someone describing it), vendor or practitioner (sells the service it describes; read for shape, not for truth). Re-verify quarterly.

- [6] [Matthew Deal: Fractional CMO scope of work, what it should actually include](https://matthewdeal.com/blog/fractional-cmo-scope-of-work). For: the fractional-CMO model the lead copies (strategy and metrics agreed in 90 days, weekly founder sync, monthly report, production out of scope). Jurisdiction: any. Kind: practitioner. Last checked 2026-11-09.
- [7] [EOS Worldwide: EOS glossary (Accountability Chart, Rocks, Scorecard, Level 10 meeting)](https://www.eosworldwide.com/glossary). For: the scorecard idea (five to fifteen numbers reviewed weekly) behind the monthly marketing scorecard. Jurisdiction: any. Kind: primary for the method's own terms (the page refuses automated fetches; open it in a browser). Last checked 2026-11-09.
- [45] [April Dunford: Books (Obviously Awesome; Sales Pitch)](https://www.aprildunford.com/books). For: the positioning method and its five components (competitive alternatives, unique attributes, value, best-fit customers, market category) used in `positioning.md`. Jurisdiction: any. Kind: primary (the author's own site; the method is in the books). Last checked 2026-11-09.
