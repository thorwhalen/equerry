# Bizdev: starter sources

Numbers are those of the REFERENCES list of the framework's research report `docs/research/domain-experts-for-a-solo-technical-founder.md`, so they match across every team's sources. Kind: primary (the authority itself), secondary (someone describing it), vendor or practitioner (sells the service it describes; read for shape, not for truth). Re-verify quarterly; a source older than a quarter makes the knowledge dimension of a coverage check stale.

- [3] [Yokoi T, Bonsall A. How Part-Time Senior Leaders Can Help Your Business. Harvard Business Review, 2024-07-02](https://hbr.org/2024/07/how-part-time-senior-leaders-can-help-your-business). For: what a fractional executive is; the model for the lead. Jurisdiction: any. Kind: secondary (only the introduction was read). Last checked 2026-11-09.
- [4] [Arc.dev: Fractional CTO, what they do, what they cost, and when a startup should hire one](https://arc.dev/employer-blog/?p=5494). For: scope, first 30 days, decision rights and out-of-scope of a fractional role; the shape of a fractional-CTO offer. Jurisdiction: any. Kind: vendor. Last checked 2026-11-09.
- [5] [Burkland Associates: What startup founders should expect from a fractional CFO service package (2026-03-17)](https://burklandassociates.com/2026/03/17/what-startup-founders-should-expect-from-a-fractional-cfo-service-package/). For: how a fractional package is scoped and what is sold separately; a model for offer pages. Jurisdiction: any (US practice). Kind: vendor. Last checked 2026-11-09.
- [6] [Matthew Deal: Fractional CMO scope of work, what it should actually include](https://matthewdeal.com/blog/fractional-cmo-scope-of-work). For: retainer scope, cadence and exclusions of a fractional engagement; a model for statements of work. Jurisdiction: any. Kind: practitioner. Last checked 2026-11-09.
- [23] [Centre Inffo: Transmission du BPF, report au 31 mai 2026](https://www.centre-inffo.fr/site-droit-formation/actualites-droit/transmission-du-bpf-report-au-31-mai-2026). For: the annual pedagogical and financial report (BPF) of a training provider and its 2026 campaign dates. Jurisdiction: FR. Kind: secondary (the reference body for training law; confirm the year's dates each spring). Last checked 2026-11-09.
- [24] [DREETS Normandie: Qualiopi, la certification qualité des organismes de formation](https://normandie.dreets.gouv.fr/QUALIOPI-la-certification-qualite-des-organismes-de-formation). For: when Qualiopi is required (training paid from public or pooled funds, since 1 January 2022) and how certification works. Jurisdiction: FR. Kind: primary (a state administration). Last checked 2026-11-09.
- [47] [MEDDICC: MEDDPICC sales methodology and process](https://meddicc.com/meddpicc-sales-methodology-and-process). For: the qualification method used in the weekly pipeline review. Jurisdiction: any. Kind: vendor (the method's own site). Last checked 2026-11-09.
