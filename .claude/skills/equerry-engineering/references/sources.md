# Engineering: starter sources

Numbers are those of the REFERENCES list of the framework's research report `docs/research/domain-experts-for-a-solo-technical-founder.md`, so they match across every team's sources. Kind: primary (the authority itself), secondary (someone describing it), vendor or practitioner. Re-verify quarterly; the Cyber Resilience Act items most of all.

- [35] [European Commission: Cyber Resilience Act, open source](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/cra-open-source). For: when free and open-source software is in or out of the CRA's scope, and the duties of an open-source steward; the basis of the scope memo. Jurisdiction: EU. Kind: primary. Last checked 2026-11-09.
- [36] [European Commission: Cyber Resilience Act, reporting obligations](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/cra-reporting). For: manufacturers' reporting of actively exploited vulnerabilities and severe incidents, and its start date (11 September 2026). Jurisdiction: EU. Kind: primary. Last checked 2026-11-09.
- [48] [DORA: DORA's software delivery metrics, the four keys](https://dora.dev/guides/dora-metrics-four-keys/). For: the four delivery numbers (deployment frequency, lead time for changes, change failure rate, time to restore) and how to measure them. Jurisdiction: any. Kind: primary (the research programme's own guide). Last checked 2026-11-09.
- [49] [OWASP: Application Security Verification Standard](https://owasp.org/www-project-application-security-verification-standard/). For: the security baseline at level 1 for anything with users' data; check which ASVS version is current before mapping requirements. Jurisdiction: any. Kind: primary. Last checked 2026-11-09.
- [50] [OpenSSF Scorecard](https://scorecard.dev/). For: automated checks of repository and dependency hygiene, by command line or the public viewer. Jurisdiction: any. Kind: primary. Last checked 2026-11-09.
- [51] [Developer Certificate of Origin](https://developercertificate.org/). For: the contributor sign-off (`Signed-off-by`) a project may require instead of a contributor licence agreement. Jurisdiction: any. Kind: primary. Last checked 2026-11-09.
- [52] [FSFE: REUSE](https://reuse.software/). For: licence and copyright information in every file, the `LICENSES/` folder and the `reuse lint` check. Jurisdiction: any. Kind: primary. Last checked 2026-11-09.
- [53] [Open Source Initiative: Licenses](https://opensource.org/licenses). For: whether a licence is OSI-approved, and its text. Jurisdiction: any. Kind: primary. Last checked 2026-11-09.
- [55] [Apple Developer: Membership renewal](https://developer.apple.com/support/renewal/). For: the annual renewal of the developer account an iOS app depends on. Jurisdiction: any. Kind: primary. Last checked 2026-11-09.

Borrowed from other teams when an engineering ask crosses into them (read them in that team's `sources.md`): legal's [32] GDPR register, [33] COPPA, [37] AI Act; finance's [54] app-store taxes.
