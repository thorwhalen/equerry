# Legal team: starter sources

Each entry: `[n]` title, URL, what it is for, jurisdiction, primary or secondary, last checked. The numbers are those of the REFERENCES list in this framework's research report `docs/research/domain-experts-for-a-solo-technical-founder.md`, so a number means the same source in every team's file. "Primary" means the statute, the regulator, the official form or the issuer of the document; "secondary" means a law firm's note, a guide or a vendor, not checked against the primary text. Re-verify quarterly; a source on a moving topic (AI Act, COPPA, CRA, beneficial ownership, QSBS) is stale after a quarter.

Some primary pages refused automated fetches on the last check (Justia, NVCA, Carta, the ABA PDF); their content rests on search-indexed text and the secondary sources named alongside. Open them by hand before calling a claim from them certain.

## Entities and filings

- [9] Delaware Division of Revenue, Franchise tax. <https://revenue.delaware.gov/franchise-tax/>. For: the annual report and franchise tax (due 1 March), the two calculation methods, minimums, penalty and interest, good standing. `[US]` Delaware. Primary. Last checked 2026-11-09.
- [10] Davis Polk, FinCEN eliminates beneficial ownership reporting requirements for U.S. companies and U.S. persons (2025). <https://www.davispolk.com/insights/client-update/fincen-eliminates-beneficial-ownership-reporting-requirements-us-companies>. For: who still reports beneficial ownership after the March 2025 interim final rule (foreign companies registered in a US state). `[US]`. Secondary (law firm). Last checked 2026-11-09.
- [12] Haven, Form D filing guide for startups. <https://www.usehaven.com/blog-posts/form-d-filing-guide-for-startups-sec-compliance-explained>. For: Form D within 15 calendar days of the first sale, SAFEs included, and state notice filings. `[US]`. Secondary. Last checked 2026-11-09.
- [16] Legalstart, Délai d'approbation des comptes. <https://www.legalstart.fr/fiches-pratiques/comptabilite-entreprise/delai-approbation-des-comptes/>. For: SAS accounts approval deadline (set by the statutes; six months for a SASU) and filing at the greffe; the primary text is C. com. art. L227-9. `[FR]`. Secondary. Last checked 2026-11-09.

## Equity, financing and conflicts

- [11] IRS Form 15620, Section 83(b) election. <https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/f15620.pdf>. For: the 30-day election for restricted stock; no late election; the form itself is optional. `[US]`. Primary. Last checked 2026-11-09.
- [13] Carta, A founder's guide to the 409A valuation. <https://carta.com/learn/startups/equity-management/409a-valuation/>. For: when a 409A is needed before option grants, its validity (up to 12 months or until a material event such as a SAFE or a priced round). `[US]`. Secondary (vendor). Last checked 2026-11-09.
- [14] Gibson Dunn, Focus on QSBS provisions in the One Big Beautiful Bill Act (2025). <https://www.gibsondunn.com/focus-on-qsbs-provisions-in-the-one-big-beautiful-bill-act/>. For: QSBS holding periods, cap and gross-asset test for stock issued after 4 July 2025. `[US]`. Secondary (law firm). Last checked 2026-11-09.
- [30] Delaware Code title 8 §122, including §122(17) (Justia). <https://law.justia.com/codes/delaware/title-8/chapter-1/subchapter-ii/section-122>. For: renunciation of corporate opportunities by charter or board action. `[US]` Delaware. Primary (statute, via Justia). Last checked 2026-11-09.
- [31] Ervin Cohen & Jessup, Delaware SB 154, a shift in the corporate opportunity doctrine. <https://www.ecjlaw.com/ecj-blog/delaware-sb-154-a-shift-in-the-corporate-opportunity-doctrine-by-jeffrey-r-glassman>. For: how the corporate-opportunity doctrine applies to a director with roles in several companies. `[US]` Delaware. Secondary (law firm). Last checked 2026-11-09.
- [38] Y Combinator, Documents (post-money SAFE and user guide). <https://www.ycombinator.com/documents>. For: the standard SAFE a draft starts from. `[US]`. Primary (issuer of the template). Last checked 2026-11-09.
- [39] NVCA, Model legal documents. <https://nvca.org/model-legal-documents/>. For: model documents for priced venture rounds. `[US]`. Primary (issuer of the templates). Last checked 2026-11-09.
- [40] Cooley GO, Documents. <https://www.cooleygo.com/documents/>. For: document generators and plain-language guides for formation, equity and financing. `[US]`. Secondary (law firm). Last checked 2026-11-09.
