# Product: starter sources

Numbers are those of the REFERENCES list of the framework's research report `docs/research/domain-experts-for-a-solo-technical-founder.md`, so they match across every team's sources. Kind: primary (the authority itself), secondary (someone describing it), vendor or practitioner (sells the service it describes; read for shape, not for truth). Re-verify quarterly.

- [43] [Basecamp: Shape Up](https://basecamp.com/shapeup). For: shaping work as a pitch (problem, appetite, solution, rabbit holes, no-gos), fixed time and variable scope, the six-week cycle and the betting table; the basis of the bet pitch. Jurisdiction: any. Kind: primary (the method's own book, free online). Last checked 2026-11-09.
- [44] [Product Talk: Opportunity solution trees](https://www.producttalk.org/opportunity-solution-trees/). For: continuous discovery, linking a desired outcome to opportunities, solutions and assumption tests; the basis of the opportunity tree. Jurisdiction: any. Kind: primary (the method's author). Last checked 2026-11-09.
- [46] [Rob Fitzpatrick: The Mom Test](https://www.momtestbook.com/). For: customer interviews that ask about past behaviour and specifics instead of opinions and the future; the basis of the interview guide. Jurisdiction: any. Kind: primary (the author's site; the rules are in the book). Last checked 2026-11-09.
