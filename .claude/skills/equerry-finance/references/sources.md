# Finance team: starter sources

Each entry: `[n]` title, URL, what it is for, jurisdiction, primary or secondary, last checked. The numbers are those of the REFERENCES list in this framework's research report `docs/research/domain-experts-for-a-solo-technical-founder.md`, so a number means the same source in every team's file. "Primary" means the tax authority, the regulator, the official form or the issuer; "secondary" means an accountant's or law firm's note, a guide or a vendor, not checked against the primary text. Re-verify quarterly: rates, thresholds and regimes change every year.

Some primary pages refused automated fetches on the last check (Carta, economie.gouv.fr); their content rests on search-indexed text. Open them by hand before calling a claim from them certain.

## United States

- [25] IRS, Important tax deadlines and dates. <https://www.irs.gov/node/144561>. For: individual estimated tax dates; contractor reporting dates (confirm the 1099-NEC date each year); weekend and holiday roll-forward. `[US]`. Primary. Last checked 2026-11-09.
- [65] FinCEN, Report foreign bank and financial accounts (FBAR). <https://www.fincen.gov/report-foreign-bank-and-financial-accounts>. For: FBAR threshold (aggregate above $10,000), due date and automatic extension; Form 8938 is a separate obligation. `[US]`. Primary. Last checked 2026-11-09.
- [15] Weaver, R&E tax relief: how the One Big Beautiful Bill Act impacts Section 174A. <https://weaver.com/resources/re-tax-relief-how-the-one-big-beautiful-bill-act-impacts-section-174a/>. For: domestic research expenditure, software development included, deductible again. `[US]`. Secondary (accounting firm). Last checked 2026-11-09.
- [13] Carta, A founder's guide to the 409A valuation. <https://carta.com/learn/startups/equity-management/409a-valuation/>. For: when a 409A is needed and how long it lasts, for the tax side of equity grants. `[US]`. Secondary (vendor). Last checked 2026-11-09.
- [63] IRS, Enrolled agents, frequently asked questions. <https://www.irs.gov/tax-professionals/enrolled-agents/frequently-asked-questions-faqs>. For: who may represent a taxpayer before the IRS (attorneys, CPAs, enrolled agents); the escalation line. `[US]`. Primary. Last checked 2026-11-09.

## France

- [18] impots.gouv.fr, Impôts locaux: CET (CFE et CVAE). <https://www.impots.gouv.fr/portail/professionnel/cet-cfe-et-cvae>. For: the CFE and its 15 December balance. `[FR]`. Primary. Last checked 2026-11-09.

For: the simplified VAT regime's annual return and instalments; the reported end of the regime on 1 January 2027 (not verified). `[FR]`. Secondary. Last checked 2026-11-09.

- [23] Centre Inffo, Transmission du BPF: report au 31 mai 2026. <https://www.centre-inffo.fr/site-droit-formation/actualites-droit/transmission-du-bpf-report-au-31-mai-2026>. Last checked 2026-11-09.
- [62] Eurojuris, Monopole des experts-comptables: Cass. crim. 21 January 2026 no. 24-81.008. <https://www.eurojuris.fr/actus-particuliers/patrimoine/construction/articles/monopole-experts-comptables-interdiction-montage-mise-a-disposition-43629.htm>.
