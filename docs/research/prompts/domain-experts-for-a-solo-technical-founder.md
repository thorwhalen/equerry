Research theme: which domains a solo technical founder needs covered when they are at once a co-founder or senior contributor in two or three start-ups, lead a client engagement that may become a company, sell consulting and training (AI integration, fractional CTO), and build their own products (an instrument-like music app, educational tools, open-source libraries), and what one very experienced generalist expert per domain would actually do for them.

Cover: the standard list of functions in a small company and in a portfolio career (legal, people/HR, marketing, sales and business development, finance and administration, product, research and development, engineering, operations, investor and board relations, personal effectiveness) and which ones matter for a portfolio founder; for each domain, what a generalist expert does month to month (the recurring tasks and their cadence), the frameworks and checklists practitioners actually use, the first references an expert should have loaded (standard documents, templates, regulatory calendars), the decisions that are the founder's alone and the ones an expert can take, and the line beyond which a licensed professional (a lawyer, an accountant, a tax adviser) is required and an AI agent must say so; the fractional executive model (fractional CTO, CMO, CFO: scope, deliverables, how they report) as the template for what an agent expert should produce; what each domain needs to know about the principal and the ventures (the dossier each team keeps); the cross-domain items (an equity grant touches legal, finance and people; a product launch touches marketing, legal and engineering) and how small companies coordinate them; how a founder tracks a portfolio of ventures (one page per venture: role, time budget, stage, what it needs this month). Deliverable: the recommended set of teams with one line of mandate each, the per-team recurring task table, the per-team starter reference list with links, the escalation line per team, the venture page template, the standard terminology, and the minimum viable version. Also anything important I did not ask.
